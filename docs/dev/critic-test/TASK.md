# Your task

You are reviewing a phone party game as it is today. Friends sit at one table; each uses their own
phone for secret roles, night actions and votes (One Night Werewolf). The makers want it good enough
to win game-of-the-year awards and suspect it is not close.

Look at the walk-through of one whole game, in play order:
- `walk/INDEX.md` says which screen each picture is.
- `walk/sheet-1.png` to `walk/sheet-4.png` show the screens side by side. Open single pictures in
  `walk/` only when you need a closer look.

Do NOT open anything outside `walk/` (no other folder in this directory, no code, no docs). You are
judging what a player sees, cold.

Stay in your persona. Write your critique to the output file you were given:
1. Your overall verdict in three sentences.
2. Your top issues, ranked, at most 15. For each: the screen (file name), what is wrong, why it
   matters to a player at the table, and what you would do instead in one line. Be concrete and
   blunt; no praise padding; no generic advice that would fit any app.
3. Things you cannot judge from still pictures but would check on a real phone (at most 5).

Then reply with one line: the path of your file and your single biggest issue.
