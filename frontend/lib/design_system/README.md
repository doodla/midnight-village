# Design system

The rebuilt screens are built from what is here. The spec is
`docs/specs/frontend/first-time-play.md` (sections 7 and 8 override the text above them); the
look is `docs/design/` (mocks, `mock-to-skin.md`, art). Keep this file true when you change
what it describes.

## First-time play pieces

The rebuilt screens follow `docs/specs/frontend/first-time-play.md` (teach once, say little). The shared pieces take plain data and callbacks and never touch the network:

- **Words**: every string a player sees is in `lib/design_system/copy/copy.dart` (`Template` for gaps); none in widgets. `test/design_system/copy/copy_budget_test.dart` holds them to section 1 mechanically: the banned list, sentence, headline, button, banner and spoken lengths, the one question, no gesture explained (`gestureKept` would list a line the spec keeps; none today), and every screen state's word budget (`screen_states.dart`). `bad_copy_fixture.dart` proves each rule fails. A new screen state is a new entry there. A unit symbol ("s") is not a word only right after a number or a gap ("14 s", "{n} s"). The night's result lines (`SawCopy`) are facts of the night, in the past ("Cleo's card was: Werewolf.", "You took Ana's card: Seer."); only the Insomniac's says "now" (`face_content_test.dart` holds it). `widget_strings_test.dart` reads the syntax of `lib/design_system/{primitives,faces,night,teach}` and fails on any string there that could reach a screen or a screen reader (the server's snake_case keys, letterless joins and developer-only messages aside).
- **Role text**: `RoleText.of(role)` (`copy/role_text.dart`), the one table for the 04 face, the role sheet (`RoleSheetBody`) and the team tags.
- **Cues**: `LearnedCues` (`teach/learned_store.dart`) keeps `mv.learned.v1.<id>` in `DevicePreferences` (beside it, once the screens branch lands, `mv.looked.v1.<stage+player>` remembers that this phone has looked at its card for a stage), read before the first frame (`restoreLearned()` in `main`). Ids: `hold` (taught once: the ring on whatever hides a secret until the first reveal on any face; then on none), `night_choose`, `vote_lock`; stored ids no longer in `Cue` (the old `hold_you`, `hold_my_night`) are ignored. The debug toolbar's "Reset learned cues" calls `ref.read(learnedCuesProvider.notifier).reset()`. `TeachOnce` shows a cue until learned, or for 3 s after its `CueReplay` fires (a tap on a locked gate); `TeachingHold` is a `HoldToReveal` with the hold cue (`HoldCue`: a plain ring with "Hold" in it that breathes and fills while held (`HoldPress`), gone once learned; no thumbprint); `CueWave` is the board's lift and the vote rows' nudge. Reduce motion makes them still.
- **Gates**: only 04's "Ready" (a 0.8 s reveal) and a button waiting for a valid pick ("Choose a card", "Choose 1 more"); `PrimaryButton.locked` replays the screen's cue. "Ready to vote" is never locked. Nothing adds a wait or assumes a timer length: lengths come from the server.
- **Secrets**: `HoldToReveal` shows its face 120 ms after the press for as long as it is held (no cap) and counts a reveal at 0.8 s; a tap is never a reveal (it reaches the cover at once: on the board, a pick); a screen reader, switch or keyboard shows it until activated again; it hides on focus loss, a covering route and the app leaving the foreground. The face is a `HeldFace`: the paper role card of mock 04 (`heroRole` for the role's name, the portrait through `ArtPicture.decodeAt`). On 04 it turns over in place like a piece of cut paper (`PaperFlip`, `primitives/paper_flip.dart`: see "Your card turns over" below); everywhere else it shows above the touch point (below it, clear of the thumb, when there is no room above), never under the thumb, sized to its words; text scale capped at 1.6, words out of semantics unless activated. Elsewhere in the screens branch: `PrimaryButton.checkMark` (a done tick), `WorldTimer.format` ("14 s" under a minute, "4:12" from a minute, `CommonCopy.seconds`; a bare number was not read as seconds), `WorldTimer` and `TimerBand` with null seconds (a stage with no deadline shows no count), `TimerBand` with no share (null `elapsed`/`remaining`: the sun rests at `restingSun`, the bar is unlit; nothing is drawn that the server did not say) and with the screen's `title` beside its number (under it where they do not fit side by side), the talk's sun kept clear of the rooftops (`_SunPainter.rise`), `PeekBox(large:)`, `FadingScroll` (a part of a `fitsHeight` body that scrolls only when it cannot fit) and a `fitsHeight` body under a `topSlot` given the height left under the band. Its content is read from `/me/` by `NightReport.fromMe` (`faces/night_report.dart`): `card` (04), `you(prompt)` ("You" at night; once her look is back, the Doppelganger's copy for the rest of window 1), `myNight` (08: the result first, window 1's page when the deck holds the Doppelganger, then "Dealt"; the same count on every phone). A face of several pages shows them all at once, stacked (the first large, the others as rows with a small portrait, `HeldFace`); the first portrait gives up more height for them (`minStackedPortraitShare`): two dots and a second hold were found by nobody. The results (`NightResult.fromEntry`, `WindowOneResult.fromEntries`) and prompt kinds (`NightPrompt.fromKind`) are parsed from the server's words, one to one: a Seer's two centre cards by their places (`center_indices`), and the viewer's own `skip` (`nothing_chosen`: chose, and nothing picked meant anything) as "You did nothing.".
- **The night board**: `NightBoard` (`night/`) takes the seats (`NightSeat.seatsOf(me)`), this phone's id, its `ChoosePrompt.fromMe(me)` (the stage, the window, the kind, and `PickRule.fromPrompt`: the prompt's `shapes` (`{"players": n}`, `{"center": n}`, `{"any": 1}`) over its `valid_targets`; "You" alone, doing nothing, is always a choice), the face of "You", the seconds left, and `onChoose(stage, picks)`, which answers a `ChooseAnswer` (`fromStatus`: `200` taken; a `409` (`wrong_stage`, `already_chosen`) stale, so the caller refetches `/me/`; any other `4xx` refused, which arms the board again with the picks lifted; no answer `notSent`, the only case that shows `Notice` "Not sent." with "Try again"). A card the prompt cannot take does not select; once a pick has begun a card that cannot join it is dimmed (`PickRule.dims`, read from the `shapes` only; nothing dims before the first tap, for every role, so the board shows no role to a neighbour: a Seer's player pick dims everything else, her center card dims the players and leaves the other two center cards lit, a Robber's player dims the center and the other players, a Drunk's center card dims the players and the other two; "You" alone is no pick and is never dimmed; the card back and its token dim (`FaceDownCard.dimmed`, the skin's `dimmed` opacity), its name stays in full ink, and a tap on a dimmed card still switches the pick: `FaceDownCard.semanticHint` says "Switches your pick", since a dimmed vote row is switched off); Choose shows "Chosen" at once and never waits; one Choose per turn, and the board re-arms only on a new stage or turn (`ChoosePrompt.turn`, read from the server's `chosen` and `chooses_left`, never from the prompt's `kind`: it changes only when the server arms another turn, the Doppelganger's follow-up); a phone whose turn the server already has (`chooses_left` 0, `ChoosePrompt.used`) starts at "Chosen". A board with nothing to choose (`ChoosePrompt.sleeps`: the prompt's `kind` is `none`, and the server sends `chooses_left` 0 and never waits for it) asks nothing: "Nothing to do" quiet in the button's place (`PrimaryButton.done`), no teaching wave, and a tap bobs a card once (`CueWave`) and picks or sends nothing. The board never scrolls (`ScreenFrame(fitsHeight: true)`) and is laid out by `BoardLayout.solve` (`night/board_layout.dart`), from the room, the text scale and every name at the table, this phone's own included (so every phone of a table gets the same board): the OTHER players' seats in balanced rows that never leave a card alone (4 others are 4 or 2 + 2, 7 are 4 + 3, 8 are 4 + 4, 9 are 3 + 3 + 3; at most five to a row; the one exception is three others, which may stand as a pair with the third centred under it, `BoardLayout.triangle`), and under them the band in front of the player: their own card ("You") on a paper place (`PaperCard`) beside the table's three cards on a `ground.panel` strip, the four one size (a seat's, or less where the width needs it; `bandCard`). Of the seats' splits it takes the one with the largest cards that keeps every name at the 14 px floor (`ReadingFloor.act`) (cards within 8% of each other: fewer rows), every card up to `BoardLayout.largest` (168: the cards use the height the phone gives them, so a small table's cards grow past the mocks' 96 and there is no dead band; past 96 each gap keeps `BoardLayout.breath`, and the token grows a little with the card, `FaceDownCard.ownerTokenGrowth`) and as small as it takes for every row to be on screen; a split that leaves more than `BoardLayout.deadBand` of the height unused in each gap loses to one that does not, whatever its cards' size (this is what takes the triangle at four players, where a row of three is as wide as the phone and leaves a tall phone empty); where no split keeps both the names at their floor and the cards at `BoardLayout.smallest` (ten long names on a 320 phone) it takes the one falling least short, the names counted twice. Every name is set at one size (`FaceDownCard.nameSize`) in the same room (`nameHeight`), on two lines between words where it must, never cut; a column is as wide as its card and a gap, or its widest name, so the seats sit together. A tap reaches a seat's whole column and a band card's cell (`bandCell`), at least `BoardLayout.minTap` (44) wide, and anywhere on the paper place for "You" (a hold there shows its face too); a split that cannot give 44 scores as falling short. The button says what will happen, by the prompt's `kind` (`NightPrompt.go` and `.pass`, the words in `NightCopy`, required of every kind: a sleeper's carry the plain "Choose {cards}" and "Skip"): "See Cleo", "See Left, Right", "Swap with Cleo", "Swap Ana, Ben", "Swap with Middle", "Copy Cleo"; the names wrap between words onto two lines and are set smaller before they are cut (`board_sweep_test.dart` puts the two longest names the server allows in every button at 320 wide). Tapping "You" alone is doing nothing and the button says what that is for the role: "Skip" (Seer, Troublemaker, Doppelganger, lone Werewolf), "Keep my card" (the Robber only), "Choose for me" (the Drunk, who is swapped by the server all the same); its hold ring loses its word only where the word would fall below the 12 px label floor (`ReadingFloor.caption` in `layout/space.dart`, which the gallery's floor reads too). The layout is solved again only when the names, the room, the text scale or the skin change, not on the countdown's tick. The strip carries the word "Center" above its cards (the titles say "center cards"; `BoardLayout.labelRoom`, and the place leaves the same room so the four cards sit level). The Seer's board (owner's choice B, 22 November 2026) says where her two kinds of pick are: `NightPrompt.seats` ("1 player") above the seats, a rule with "or" in the gap above the band, `NightPrompt.strip` ("2 center cards") in place of "Center". Every board keeps that room (`BoardLayout.seatLabelRoom`, `ruleRoom`) and leaves it empty, so no card sits elsewhere for any role; the room comes from the row gap and the band's padding (4 px tighter) and from the header's gap to the board being the label room; "You" has one line of room under its card whatever the table's names need (`youNameHeight`), and the table's cards and "You" reach 8 px down into the band's padding for a tap (`FaceDownCard.tapExtra`), so every tap target stays 4 px over 48 at 320 x 568 with ten players. A prompt kind this app does not know is `NightPrompt.generic` ("Choose a card", "Choose {cards}", "Skip"), an unknown shape is left out of `PickRule` (none left: any one card), both logged: never a throw in a player's hand. The table's cards are dropped at the scatter tilts; what is to spare (little: the cards grew to the room) goes in equal shares above the seats, between the rows and between them and the band (the rule with "or" in the middle of that last one), so no gap is a dead band (`board_composition_test.dart`: every gap holds what the design puts in it and at most 40 px more, 72 at three seats where two cards are as wide as the phone allows (the 64 px timer line gives the 8 px the smaller title left); none where the cards are already at their smallest). ## The skin: one look, values from the skin

There is one look today: **Shadow paper** (from `docs/design/mocks/S*.dc.html`). A future look is a separate design with its own layouts, components and original art, not a recolouring of these screens. The values still come from the skin, so nothing is hard-coded: the look lives in a `Skin` (`lib/design_system/skin/`) holding per-ground colour roles and hard offset shadows (`Ground`: night, day, and the paper inside a card), type roles (`SkinType`), shape (`SkinShape`), tilt roles (`SkinTilt`, picked with `Tilt.card`, `Tilt.scatter(i)`, ...), the scrim, the dimmed opacity and the art set. Spacing and motion are not in it: `Space` and `Motion` in `lib/design_system/layout/space.dart`, read as `Space.md` with no skin in reach. The registry is `SkinId` (`skin_id.dart`), with the one entry `shadowPaper`.

- Build screens from the primitives in `lib/design_system/primitives/` and read values through `context.skin`, `context.ground` and `Space`/`Motion`, never literals. `docs/design/mock-to-skin.md` maps every element of every mock to the primitive and role that draws it; `test/helpers/component_sheet.dart` rebuilds each mock that way (the pages `join` to `results`), so copy from there. - Grounds are always declared: the app root declares night (`App`), `ScreenFrame`, `PaperCard`, the bands and `PeekBox` declare theirs, and `GroundScope` is an `InheritedTheme`, so sheets and dialogs carry the ground they were opened on. `GroundScope.of` asserts when there is none (tests: `pumpSkinned`).
- Type: the brief's scale (design brief section 4) with one named hero role per screen (`heroTitle`, `heroCode`, `heroVerdict`, `heroWinner`); `skin_contract_test.dart` holds the skin to it and every new role must be given a kind there.
- Amber is the one warm accent: one amber fill per screen, the primary action (`ground.accent`), edged in ink by day (`ground.accentEdge`). A selection is lift plus an outline (`ground.selection`), never amber or a fill. `amber_rule_test.dart` counts the amber fills on every component-sheet page.

## Scene changes (the dusk and the dawn)

`SceneSwitcher<T>` (`primitives/scene_switcher.dart`) is the table shell's one way to change screen. It holds the arriving screen and, for a change of `stage`, the leaving one, and runs a crossfade (`Motion.settle`: the new screen fades in over the old, which stays whole) unless `sceneBetween` names a `SceneKind`, then a scene (`Motion.scene`, 500 ms). The shell names two: **dusk** (02 or 03 to 04 or the board) and **dawn** (04 or the board to 08). Vote to count and every other change stay crossfades. Reduce motion (`Motion.within`, which the switcher reads): an instant cut, same information. The leaving screen takes no touch, no semantics and no Back (`BackMute`: Back goes to the arriving screen). A full-screen tap that only finishes a motion (10, 11) is no button to a screen reader (`excludeFromSemantics`); every button primitive (`PrimaryButton`, `SecondaryButton`, `TextAction`) carries its own tap in its semantics, so a screen reader can press it.

A scene is made of nothing but the two screens and a paper edge, no picture of its own and no dissolve: one screen is a sheet of paper that moves on a hard edge (a lip in the night's `paperEdge`, `SkinShape.paperLip`, and a hard `shadow`, `SkinShape.paperShadow` at its widest), over the first 85% of the scene (`Motion.curve`); the other stays in place.
- **Dusk, the night falls**: the card's screen is lowered over the table's from above, its foot leading on the lip and a shadow that shortens as it lands, while the table under it dims to half (`SceneSwitcher.dusk`); the card's header (04's layered village, `ScreenFrame.topScene`) comes down further than the sheet (`ScreenFrame.arrivalTravel`, 30% of its height), and in it, each in its own window of the scene's 500 ms (`SceneLayer.span`, over `SceneArrival.scene`, the scene's own linear clock): the clouds drift 16 canvas px into place from 150 to 425 ms, and last, from 250 to 500 ms, the moon is lowered into its sky from above the top edge (220 canvas px, 110 on a phone), so it is still coming when the sheet is within 2 px of rest (`scene_shell_test.dart` holds it).
- **Dawn, the day breaks**: the night sheet sinks off the screen on its top edge, its shadow thinning as it goes; the talk, whole and live underneath, has its own header come into its window (`TimerBand.sun`, the layered dawn clipped to the sky's window on the night's ground): the hills, the middle roofs and the gallows on them rise from 300 canvas px below as one bank (so no edge of one shows free of what hides it), the near roofs 12 px further, the birds drift in, and the sun comes up from behind the hills to where the clock puts it; the sky stays and the text under it does not move.
The header pictures know the scene by `SceneArrival` (`primitives/scene_arrival.dart`): an inherited 0-to-1, eased (`progress`: the sheet's landing), and the scene's own clock (`scene`, linear over `Motion.scene`), only for the arriving screen while a scene runs; `ArrivalShift` moves a picture by its own height times what is left of it, and `LayeredArt` moves each layer from its `from` to rest (see below). A screen with no scene running is told nothing and sits where it rests. No layer is ever seen through another (a pixel test reads every frame of both scenes with flat screens and fails on a blend or on a pixel no widget painted). The primary button is on show by 60% of the scene (tested on both).

Rules the switcher keeps, each tested in `scene_switcher_test.dart` and `scene_shell_test.dart`: the arriving screen is in the tree and takes touches from the first frame, **where it will rest** (its slide uses `transformHitTests: false`; the leaving screen and the edge are `IgnorePointer` and out of semantics); nothing waits, blocks or delays a phase change (a scene is a slide of layers already painted, `RepaintBoundary` under each); a screen keeps its state from arriving to leaving (the same shape of widgets per layer, a `GlobalKey` each, so it can move into the sheet); a change that comes while a scene runs (05's wait giving way to the card) swaps the arriving screen in place and does not restart the scene; a scene that starts while a crossfade runs takes both of its screens into the sheet, which finish fading inside it (each change has its own controller). While `/me/` loads the shell shows the destination's own frame (`_CardWaits`: 04's village and headline, for the night's first screen after the deal; `_TalkWaits`: 08's sky, its dotted path and the headline, and no sun: where the talk is is not known until `/me/` says, and a sun drawn at the start would be seen leaving as the real one came), so a scene never lands on an empty ground and the screen then crossfades in on the same header. **Layered headers** (`LayeredArt`, `primitives/layered_art.dart`; the description is `LayeredScene` in `skin/art_set.dart`, the data in `shadow_paper.dart`; the art and how far each piece may move: `docs/design/art/layers/PROVENANCE.md`). A layer is a cut of paper with alpha, laid at its anchor on the canvas the layers were cut from, scaled and cropped exactly as the flat picture was (`SceneGeometry.cover`: cover, around the scene's focus), so at rest the layers are that flat picture (`layered_scene_test.dart` holds them to it). Brought in by a scene each layer travels from its `from` (canvas px) to rest in `Motion.curve` like everything else, over the sheet's landing, or, with a `span` (a share of the scene), in a window of its own, so one can arrive after another. Nothing moves by itself: there is no ticker and nothing idles, so a screen with it settles and night stays still. A shadow cast on another layer (`clipTo`: the clouds' on the moon) is drawn over it only where it has paint (`BlendMode.srcATop` in a layer the size of the moon, `_SrcAtopStack`), moving with its cloud, not with the moon. `LayeredArt` is its own repaint boundary, so the hold ring breathing on 04 draws the header once, not every frame. 04: the plate, the clouds' shadows on the sky, the moon, the shadows on the moon, the clouds; the clouds (and their shadows, on the sky and on the moon) start 16 px left (dark) or right (cream), the moon 220 px up. 08: the sky, the sun and its dotted path (`SceneSlot('sun')`: drawn by `TimerBand`, under the hills, so the path's ends and the sun at the start and end of the day are behind them), the far hills, the birds, the middle roofs, the gallows, the near roofs. The sun is the paper sprite on the path (`SceneArc`, in canvas pixels, the control raised so the sun clears the far ridge): `rise` (0.33) of the way along at 0 of the talk (just clear of the ridge), `set` (0.79) at 1 (60% of the disc still in view over the roofs: the timer's picture is not gone before time is up; `layered_scene_test.dart` measures it against the art), stepped by the clock with no tween; only a scene brings it up from `dip` (458) canvas px below, behind the hills. Nothing is added to a layer by the screen: reduce motion shows the layers at rest, instantly (no scene runs).

Weight: the layers are `assets/skins/shadow_paper/layers/night_village/` (8 files, 129 KB) and `assets/skins/shadow_paper/layers/dawn_gallows/` (7 files, 160 KB), each cut to the box it paints (a cloud is a cloud's pixels), 6 MB and 9 MB decoded. They replace the two flat pictures (134 KB, 6 MB decoded) and the unused dawn rooftops (44 KB): in the web build +110 KB of art and +8 KB of script net, 15 MB decoded instead of 6 MB once both are in use. `TableShell` decodes them before the scene that needs them (`LayeredArt.precache`, not awaited, at the sizes the screens draw): the night's when the shell first shows, the day's once this phone is at the night, so no one fetches the dawn before the deal. Frames of both in motion: `just scene-frames` (`test_screens/scene_frames_test.dart`); the browser check: `tool/scene_web_check.sh` (both in `test/README.md`).
