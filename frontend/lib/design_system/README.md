# Design system

The rebuilt screens are built from what is here. The spec is
`docs/specs/frontend/first-time-play.md` (sections 7 and 8 override the text above them); the
look is `docs/design/` (mocks, `mock-to-skin.md`, art). Keep this file true when you change
what it describes.

## First-time play pieces

The rebuilt screens follow `docs/specs/frontend/first-time-play.md` (teach once, say little). The shared pieces take plain data and callbacks and never touch the network:

- **Words**: every string a player sees is in `lib/design_system/copy/copy.dart` (`Template` for gaps); none in widgets. `test/design_system/copy/copy_budget_test.dart` holds them to section 1 mechanically: the banned list, sentence, headline, button, banner and spoken lengths, the one question, no gesture explained (`gestureKept` lists the one line the spec keeps), and every screen state's word budget (`screen_states.dart`). `bad_copy_fixture.dart` proves each rule fails. A new screen state is a new entry there. `widget_strings_test.dart` reads the syntax of `lib/design_system/{primitives,faces,night,teach}` and fails on any string there that could reach a screen or a screen reader (the server's snake_case keys, letterless joins and developer-only messages aside).
- **Role text**: `RoleText.of(role)` (`copy/role_text.dart`), the one table for the 04 face, the role sheet (`RoleSheetBody`) and the team tags.
- **Cues**: `LearnedCues` (`teach/learned_store.dart`) keeps `mv.learned.v1.<id>` in `DevicePreferences` (beside it, once the screens branch lands, `mv.looked.v1.<stage+player>` remembers that this phone has looked at its card for a stage), read before the first frame (`restoreLearned()` in `main`). Ids: `hold` (any reveal), `hold_you` ("You" on the night board), `hold_my_night` (08), `night_choose`, `vote_lock`. The debug toolbar's "Reset learned cues" calls `ref.read(learnedCuesProvider.notifier).reset()`. `TeachOnce` shows a cue until learned, or for 3 s after its `CueReplay` fires (a tap on a locked gate); `TeachingHold` is a `HoldToReveal` with the hold cue (`HoldCue`: a plain ring with "Hold" in it that breathes and fills while held (`HoldPress`), gone once learned; no thumbprint); `CueWave` is the board's lift and the vote rows' nudge. Reduce motion makes them still.
- **Gates**: only 04's "Ready" (a 0.8 s reveal) and a button waiting for a valid pick ("Choose a card", "Choose 1 more"); `PrimaryButton.locked` replays the screen's cue. "Ready to vote" is never locked. Nothing adds a wait or assumes a timer length: lengths come from the server.
- **Secrets**: `HoldToReveal` shows its face 120 ms after the press for as long as it is held (no cap) and counts a reveal at 0.8 s; a tap is never a reveal (it reaches the cover at once: on the board, a pick); a screen reader, switch or keyboard shows it until activated again; it hides on focus loss, a covering route and the app leaving the foreground. The face is a `HeldFace`: the paper role card of mock 04 (`heroRole` for the role's name, the portrait through `ArtPicture.decodeAt`). On 04 it turns over in place; everywhere else it shows above the touch point (below it, clear of the thumb, when there is no room above), never under the thumb, sized to its words; text scale capped at 1.6, words out of semantics unless activated. Elsewhere in the screens branch: `PrimaryButton.checkMark` (a done tick), `WorldTimer` and `TimerBand` with null seconds (a stage with no deadline shows no count), `TimerBand` with no share (null `elapsed`/`remaining`: the sun rests at `restingSun`, the bar is unlit; nothing is drawn that the server did not say) and with the screen's `title` beside its number (under it where they do not fit side by side), the talk's sun kept clear of the rooftops (`_SunPainter.rise`), `PeekBox(large:)`, `FadingScroll` (a part of a `fitsHeight` body that scrolls only when it cannot fit) and a `fitsHeight` body under a `topSlot` given the height left under the band. Its content is read from `/me/` by `NightReport.fromMe` (`faces/night_report.dart`): `card` (04), `you(prompt)` ("You" at night; once her look is back, the Doppelganger's copy for the rest of window 1), `myNight` (08: the result first, window 1's page when the deck holds the Doppelganger, then "Dealt"; the same count on every phone). The results (`NightResult.fromEntry`, `WindowOneResult.fromEntries`) and prompt kinds (`NightPrompt.fromKind`) are parsed from the server's words, one to one: a Seer's two centre cards by their places (`center_indices`), and the viewer's own `skip` (`nothing_chosen`: chose, and nothing picked meant anything) as "You did nothing.".
- **The night board**: `NightBoard` (`night/`) takes the seats (`NightSeat.seatsOf(me)`), this phone's id, its `ChoosePrompt.fromMe(me)` (the stage, the window, the kind, and `PickRule.fromPrompt`: the prompt's `shapes` (`{"players": n}`, `{"center": n}`, `{"any": 1}`) over its `valid_targets`; "You" alone, doing nothing, is always a choice), the face of "You", the seconds left, and `onChoose(stage, picks)`, which answers a `ChooseAnswer` (`fromStatus`: `200` taken; a `409` (`wrong_stage`, `already_chosen`) stale, so the caller refetches `/me/`; any other `4xx` refused, which arms the board again with the picks lifted; no answer `notSent`, the only case that shows `Notice` "Not sent." with "Try again"). A card the prompt cannot take does not select; Choose shows "Chosen" at once and never waits; one Choose per turn, and the board re-arms only on a new stage or turn (`ChoosePrompt.turn`, read from the server's `chosen` and `chooses_left`, never from the prompt's `kind`: it changes only when the server arms another turn, the Doppelganger's follow-up); a phone whose turn the server already has (`chooses_left` 0, `ChoosePrompt.used`) starts at "Chosen". The board never scrolls (`ScreenFrame(fitsHeight: true)`) and is laid out by `BoardLayout.solve` (`night/board_layout.dart`), from the room, the text scale and every name at the table, this phone's own included (so every phone of a table gets the same board): the OTHER players' seats in balanced rows that never leave a card alone (4 others are 4 or 2 + 2, 7 are 4 + 3, 8 are 4 + 4, 9 are 3 + 3 + 3; at most five to a row), and under them the band in front of the player: their own card ("You") on a paper place (`PaperCard`) beside the table's three cards on a `ground.panel` strip, the four one size (a seat's, or less where the width needs it; `bandCard`). Of the seats' splits it takes the one with the largest cards that keeps every name at the 14 px floor (cards within 8% of each other: fewer rows), every card up to `FaceDownSize.board` and as small as it takes for every row to be on screen; where no split keeps both the names at their floor and the cards at `BoardLayout.smallest` (ten long names on a 320 phone) it takes the one falling least short, the names counted twice. Every name is set at one size (`FaceDownCard.nameSize`) in the same room (`nameHeight`), on two lines between words where it must, never cut; a column is as wide as its card and a gap, or its widest name, so the seats sit together. A tap reaches a seat's whole column and a band card's cell (`bandCell`), at least `BoardLayout.minTap` (44) wide, and anywhere on the paper place for "You" (a hold there shows its face too); a split that cannot give 44 scores as falling short. Tapping "You" alone is doing nothing ("Keep my card"); its hold ring loses its word only where the word would fall below the 14 px caption floor (`ReadingFloor` in `layout/space.dart`, which the gallery's floor reads too). The layout is solved again only when the names, the room, the text scale or the skin change, not on the countdown's tick. The table's cards are dropped at the scatter tilts; what is to spare goes half above the seats, half between them and the band. The title is one line, set smaller only where it must, beside the candle and the seconds as one unit `timerLines` title lines high. The board's words grow with the text size to `NightBoard.labelScale` (1.3), the button's to `buttonScale` (1.6). `board_sweep_test.dart` holds every table from 3 to 10 seats, with short names, long ones and three-word ones that wrap, on 320 x 692, 320 x 568, 360 x 779, 360 x 640 and 390 x 844 phones at text x1/1.3/2, to no seat card alone on a row, every name whole, every card on screen and every tap target 44 or more; the fit sweep checks the board pages (`actionThree`, `actionTenLong` with long names among them). Elsewhere a body that runs past the button fades at its lower edge. Tests build every night input from the recorded protocol-2 samples (`recordedMe`, `Viewer` in `test/helpers/sheet/sheet_data.dart`), never by hand: the results, seats, rule and prompt have no public constructor, and `night_fixture_sources_test.dart` catches the rest. `night_layout_test.dart` holds the board's layout and motion to be the same on every phone of a recorded game and what the table sees after Choose to be the same whatever the answer; `night_board_test.dart` and `pick_rule_test.dart` cover the rest.
- **The look**: one amber fill per screen (`amber_rule_test.dart`), the reading floor (16 px to act, 14 px captions; `just screens ... strict` fails a player screen below it), plain pips, no uppercase micro-labels.
- **Pictures**: the component sheet's screen pages are these pieces in each state (`component_sheet_states_test.dart` checks each page reaches its state); a page is a phone of a recorded game (`ComponentPage.viewer`). The 10 and 11 pages are those screens' own views (`CountView`, `ResultsView`) fed by the recorded results bodies (`test/helpers/outcome_samples.dart`: the Hunter's chain, the Tanner, ten long names; the Werewolves winning and nobody dying derived from a recording by its votes until one is recorded), so the sweeps measure the real screens. `just playtest-pack` writes the cold-read pack: the host, guest, sleeper and Seer journeys through recorded game 7, each phone's own taps and faces, with what it has learned carried from screen to screen (`ComponentPage.teaches`), as `build/playtest/<journey>/screen-NN.png` (`test_screens/playtest_pack_test.dart`); the host's and guest's 01 to 03b are the app's real screens on recorded lobby bodies (`test_screens/table_fixtures.dart`).

## The skin: one look, values from the skin

There is one look today: **Shadow paper** (from `docs/design/mocks/S*.dc.html`). A future look is a separate design with its own layouts, components and original art, not a recolouring of these screens. The values still come from the skin, so nothing is hard-coded: the look lives in a `Skin` (`lib/design_system/skin/`) holding per-ground colour roles and hard offset shadows (`Ground`: night, day, and the paper inside a card), type roles (`SkinType`), shape (`SkinShape`), tilt roles (`SkinTilt`, picked with `Tilt.card`, `Tilt.scatter(i)`, ...), the scrim, the dimmed opacity and the art set. Spacing and motion are not in it: `Space` and `Motion` in `lib/design_system/layout/space.dart`, read as `Space.md` with no skin in reach. The registry is `SkinId` (`skin_id.dart`), with the one entry `shadowPaper`.

- Build screens from the primitives in `lib/design_system/primitives/` and read values through `context.skin`, `context.ground` and `Space`/`Motion`, never literals. `docs/design/mock-to-skin.md` maps every element of every mock to the primitive and role that draws it; `test/helpers/component_sheet.dart` rebuilds each mock that way (the pages `join` to `results`), so copy from there. Primitives: `ScreenFrame` (`fitsHeight` for a body that never scrolls; with `topArt` + `TopArtLayout` (`fill`: the picture takes the height the body leaves, the body at its foot; 01), or a full-bleed `topSlot`), `Band`, `TimerBand.sun`/`.bar`, `WorldTimer.candle`/`.candleCompact`, `PrimaryButton` (`.locked`, `.quiet`, `.done`: a sunk panel with a check before the words, no outline, so "Chosen", "Locked" and "Ready ✓" never read as a locked gate)/`SecondaryButton`(`.paper`) at a `ButtonSize`, `SkinField.paper`/`.code`, `PaperCard`, `Pill`, `PresetChip`, `StatusDot`, `TagChip`, `CountBadge`, `OutlinePanel`, `Notice`, `PaceTile`, `PeekBox`, `PlayerToken` (a `TokenSize`), `PlayerRow` (a `PlayerRowSize`, with `Tally`; its `detail`, the voters' names, wraps between words), `RoleCard` (thumbnails and grid), `RoleSheetBody`, `RolePortrait`, `FaceDownCard` (a `FaceDownSize`; `cardWidth` for a card narrower than its name; names and captions wrap, never cut), `ProgressPips`, `QrPicture` (a QR code in the ground's ink, 02's join link), `HoldToReveal`, `SheetScaffold`/`showSkinSheet`/`showSkinDialog`, `FitLine` (one line, set smaller; `alignment` for a line in a wider box, a field's hint), `FitWords` (wraps between words, never inside one; with `maxLines`, set smaller until it fits them, never an ellipsis; the buttons, labels, chips, captions and names use it), `ArtPicture`. Fixed sizes are maximums: thumbnails, face-down cards and the candle shrink to their parent.
- Grounds are always declared: the app root declares night (`App`), `ScreenFrame`, `PaperCard`, the bands and `PeekBox` declare theirs, and `GroundScope` is an `InheritedTheme`, so sheets and dialogs carry the ground they were opened on. `GroundScope.of` asserts when there is none (tests: `pumpSkinned`).
- Type: the brief's scale (design brief section 4) with one named hero role per screen (`heroTitle`, `heroCode`, `heroVerdict`, `heroWinner`); `skin_contract_test.dart` holds the skin to it and every new role must be given a kind there.
- Amber is the one warm accent: one amber fill per screen, the primary action (`ground.accent`), edged in ink by day (`ground.accentEdge`). A selection is lift plus an outline (`ground.selection`), never amber or a fill. `amber_rule_test.dart` counts the amber fills on every component-sheet page.
- Layout code never asks which skin is active: no `SkinId`, no skin comparisons outside `skin/skins/`, the registry, the app theme and `main` (`skin_identity_test.dart` scans `lib/` for every registry name).
- The Material theme comes from the skin (`skinTheme`, `appThemeProvider`, always `SkinId.fallback`); `Skin.lerp` never blends. There is no skin switch and no saved skin. The debug toolbar's "Reset learned cues" forgets what this device has been taught.
- Fonts: Gloock and Figtree, under `fonts:` in pubspec.yaml (the engine loads them at start); only the weights the type roles use ship. Their OFL licences are registered for the licences page in `lib/main.dart`.
- Art: `assets/skins/shadow_paper/{roles,tokens,scenes}/*.webp`, at the pixel sizes the `ArtSet` declares (the contract test decodes each to check). Every picture is drawn through `ArtPicture`, which decodes it at the size it is shown (`ArtImage.decodedFor`: the full size, a half or a quarter). At start `SkinLoader.prepare` runs `precacheArt(secretArt(skin))`, decoding every role portrait and the card back at the sizes secrets show them, so the first hold is never empty and a reveal fetches nothing (a role-specific fetch at reveal time would be a side channel on shared Wi-Fi; `art_test.dart` checks). All ten tokens are drawn; a token id this app does not know (`TokenId.fromServer` gives null) is a plain disc.
- Regenerating the art: `just skin-art` (`uv run --with pillow python tool/skin_art.py`) resizes the originals in `docs/design/art/`. Deterministic: run it twice and nothing changes.
- Tests on the skin: `skin_contract_test.dart` (scale, faces, art sizes, shapes, the accent and selection rules), `amber_rule_test.dart`, `contrast_test.dart` (WCAG AA measured on the painted pixels of every component-sheet page on every ground: text against the colour actually behind it, with the opacity above it; disabled controls exempt, busy ones not), `fit_sweep_test.dart` (every page at 320/360/390 wide and text x1/1.3/2: no overflow, no word broken across two lines, no text drawn cut short (`test/helpers/truncated_text.dart`: past a line limit, wider or taller than its own box with an ellipsis, a fade or a clip, or glyphs lost to a clip round it), every board card on screen (`fit_checks.dart`)), the goldens.
