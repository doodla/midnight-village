# The game: the data layer and screens 04 to 11 (`features/game`)

There is one game flow: the screens read one model and one table.
- **`/me/`**: `GameMe` (`features/game/domain/game_me.dart`), strict, with this phone's own `player_id` (never taken from the login); it carries `stageStartedAt` (`stage_started_at`: when the timed stage began, null whenever the deadline is) and, in the vote, `voteTargets` (`vote_targets`: the ids this phone may vote for, in seat order), which no screen reads yet. Its night is the design system's parsers run once on the same body (`ChoosePrompt.fromMe`, `NightSeat.seatsOf`, `NightReport.fromMe`): they ARE the night models, there is no second parser. Counts are `StageProgress`, the one parser for `/me/`'s `progress` and the `progress` event. Results: `GameOutcome` (`gameResultFromServer` plus `NightTimeline`). A `/me/` without a `stage` key is a broken payload (`GameMe.fromJson` throws): a failed fetch, healed like any other, the taps in flight kept. Play again or Leave answered 404 is the table closing (01, "This table has closed."), as a fetch answered 404 is. Lobby: `LobbyPlayer.token`, `LobbyPlayer.connected`, `LobbyState.table` (`TableSettings`: auto deck, chip, look, lengths), `LobbyState.deckPreview` and `LobbyState.deal` (`DealReadiness`: the server's own "can be dealt now"; every table sends both); `MyTable`, `OnlineStats`, `RecentGame` (`lobby/domain/where_am_i.dart`).
- **Layering**: `features/game/domain` imports the night models from `design_system/night` and `design_system/faces` (they are the parsers, so the domain does not copy them); nothing in `design_system` imports a feature.
- **Screen**: `screenFor(TableView, me:, local:)` (`game/domain/screen_table.dart`) is the one server-state -> screen table (01 to 11; 05 is the board's place while `/me/` loads; a table naming no game of this player's is 02). `tableScreenProvider` (`game/presentation/table_screen.dart`) applies it: the game id comes from `/me/table/` (a new deal replaces the game), values held during a refetch are used (never loading or error while something is shown), a game that is gone is 01. The only non-server inputs are the `LocalSteps` in `localStepsProvider` (03 opened, "See who won"), each recorded with the table state it was taken in and void in any other, so a deal, play again or new game ends them; "See who won" is also kept on the device (`mv.won.v1.<game>`, read before the first frame by `restoreLocalSteps`), so a restart at the results lands on 10 or 11 as this phone left it. `screen_table_test.dart` maps every recorded `/me/` to the screen stated for it (`meScreens` in `test/helpers/contract/sample_sets.dart`; a new sample must be added there), every where-am-I answer and lobby body, and checks every screen is reached.
- **State in**: `LiveGameNotifier` (`liveGameProvider(gameId)`). `/me/` fetches go one at a time (a request meanwhile runs once after), so a reply never overtakes a newer one. One rule for every fetch a screen depends on: it is retried with backoff until it succeeds, or polled until its reason ends (`core/healing/healing.dart`, `HealTiming`): any failed `/me/` (the first, or the one after a taken choose) is fetched again; the results (`GameOutcome`) until they load (`outcomeFailing` while a try has failed); `myTableProvider` with `retryForever` (Riverpod's default gives up after about 40 s) and, at a waiting table or a finished game, a slow poll (`HealTiming.tablePoll`) for a lost deal or play-again event. Riverpod's own retry is off on the notifier (`noRetry`): it retries itself, and a rebuild would drop the taps in flight. Events (`GameEvent`) only ask for `/me/` (and `/me/table/`) again or update a value `/me/` holds in the same absolute form (progress, host via `player_id`, presence). Reconnect and app resume (`appResumesProvider`) fetch both; after a stage's deadline, by the server's clock (the last answer's `Date` plus the time since; the phone's clock only when no `Date` is readable, and then only to time a fetch), one fetch, then one every `HealTiming.poll` while the stage is unchanged; `/me/table/` and `/me/` are each fetched again when the other is ahead. A later good fetch clears `tableClosed`; a 404 also sets 01's banner ("This table has closed.", `JoinFlow.tableClosed`), where the phone lands. The game sockets (`gameWebSocketProvider` and its stream and state, `game/presentation/game_socket.dart`) are autoDispose: a game no longer shown closes its socket.
- **Taps out**: one `Outbox` (`game/presentation/outbox.dart`), lanes choose, vote (pick and lock, in order), ready and ack: at most one request per lane in flight, later taps coalescing to the latest (queued), each item keyed to its stage (a choose: stage and turn) and holding its ORIGINAL request; an item of a stage or turn that ended is dropped, unsent ones included. What the screens show of this phone's taps is derived from it (`Outbox.on`), on the tap. Answers (`StageReply`, `StageAnswer` = `ChooseAnswer`): `taken` keeps the tap shown until a `/me/` fetched after it lands (after a taken choose every phone fetches once, so the Doppelganger's follow-up turn arms her board); an echo (`ready`, `target_player_id`) that differs fetches; `stale` (409) keeps it shown until the refetch lands; `refused` drops it; `notSent` keeps it with "Try again" (`retry(lane)`, `LiveGame.unsent`), which fetches `/me/` first and resends the original only if it did not land (a choose: `prompt.chosen > request.chosen`); the results ack has no "Try again": it is sent again by itself with backoff, and whether it is done is read from `/me/`'s `night_results_seen`, so a restart does not resend it. After ANY taken choose every phone re-reads `/me/` and trusts its `chosen` and `chooses_left` (contract, section 5). A choose is never sent twice for a turn. A tap the phone does not send is a `TapOutcome` of its own (`Declined`, `Queued`, `Landed`), never a made-up server answer; `TapOutcome.forBoard` gives the night board its `ChooseAnswer`.
- **Known and accepted**: a request whose answer was lost but which the server processes later can land after a newer tap of the same lane; closing that needs a per-phone sequence number on the server.
- **Requests**: `GameService.fetchMe`/`fetchMeTimed`, `send(StageRequest)` (`ChooseRequest.of(prompt, picks)`, which sends the prompt's `chosen` (required by the server: a lower one is a resend, answered alike and stored nowhere), `ReadyRequest`, `VoteRequest`, `AckRequest`; each echoes `stage`), `fetchOutcome`, and the host's `advanceStage`/`endTalk`/`revealVotesAt` (kept: the contract has them; no rebuilt screen calls them). `LobbyService.createTable` (`defaults: "v2"`, the only way a table is opened), `setDeckChip`, `setCustomDeck`, `setPace`, `patchSettings`, `setReady`, `fetchMyTable`, `fetchRecentGames`; `HomeService.fetchOnline` (no login); `myTableProvider`.

**The shell.** `TableShell` hosts every screen, at home (`/`, through `HomeGate`) and on a game's link (`/game/<id>`), and only `tableScreenProvider` decides which one shows: 01 to 03 are `features/lobby` (its README); a game screen is built only from a `/me/` of its own phase (09 only from a voting `/me/`, and so on): while the table names a later phase than the `/me/` held, the screen shows its ground with nothing on it until its own `/me/` lands, never the earlier phase's values; `StageSeconds` gives a screen the seconds left of its stage from the server's deadline (none when the stage has none), and `StageShare.left` how much of it is left for the sun and the vote's bar, from `/me/`'s `stage_started_at` and `deadline` (the same on every phone and after a restart); null while the server does not send `stage_started_at`, and then nothing of a share is drawn.

**08 Talk and 09 Vote** (`game/presentation/shell/talk_screen.dart`, `game/presentation/shell/vote_screen.dart`). Each is a wired screen (`TalkScreen`, `VoteScreen`: `/me/` with the outbox on it, the countdown, the notifier's taps) over a view that takes plain data and callbacks (`TalkView`, `VoteView`; the component sheet's 08 and 09 pages and so the cold-read pack draw these views). 08: the sun and the number with "The village wakes" beside it (`TimerBand.sun(title:)`), My night large (`TeachingHold` over `PeekBox(large: true)`, `NightReport.myNight`, the `hold_my_night` cue; the first counted reveal sends the results ack, `acknowledgeNightResults`, which declines once done), the deck (each role once as a small portrait with its name, "3 Villagers" where there are several; a tap or a hold opens the role sheet), plain pips and the count in full ink, "Ready to vote" quiet and never locked, a toggle through the ready lane (`setReady(!ready)`). No names: the vote lists them. 09: "Who dies today?" beside the number in the band (`TimerBand.bar(title:)`); the rows are `VoteView.votable(me)`: `/me/`'s `vote_targets` when sent, else everyone but this phone (drop that fallback once the server sends it); sized to the room (`VoteRows`: tall rows, compact rows, then a fading scroll, measuring each name at the text size; each row keeps the lift's room out of the gap so a lifted row leaves the gaps even); a tap picks (`pickVote`), "Lock in {name}" locks (`lockVote`, after the picks before it on the vote lane); locked, the pick stays lifted and outlined and the others dim; `my_vote` from `/me/` restores picked or locked; the `vote_lock` cue is learned when the server has the lock (the lane's lock confirming, or `/me/` locked), not on the tap, since a lock can wait behind a pick. Both count this phone's own ready or lock at once (`GameMe.withReady`, `withVote` move `progress` against the flag of the same `/me/`); a `progress` event that arrives while that tap is unconfirmed fetches `/me/` instead of being applied, so the count and the flag always come from one answer. Both show "Not sent." with "Try again" only for their own lane's unsent tap.

**10 and 11 (`lib/features/game/presentation/shell/count_screen.dart`, `lib/features/game/presentation/shell/results_screen.dart`).** Drawn from the results body alone, through `lib/features/game/domain/outcome_view.dart`: `TheCount` (the votes grouped by whom they named, most first; who did not vote; who died by the vote and by the Hunter, from `is_eliminated` and `elimination_reason`), `EndSeat` (each player's dealt and final card, a Doppelganger's copied role from the timeline's `copy` step, team and outcome as the server says), `Winners` (`winning_teams`), `nightLinesOf` (the timeline as one line per step, with the cards it showed or moved; werewolf cards by the server's `werewolf`). Nothing applies a rule; red is the server's `werewolf` flag on a card, everywhere (`EndSeat.werewolfNow`, `NightLine.werewolf`, `werewolfPathsOf`), and a death with no reason fails loudly. 10: the dead card first (the player's name and "Now" from the start; as it turns, the final card large with its team, "Dealt" and an arrow before it when it moved; several dead: smaller cards, "Dealt"/"Now" said once), then the rows, outlined only for the players the vote killed ("You" on this phone's own row and dead card: `CountView.viewerId`), and "{name}, {hunter}'s pick, dies too." for the Hunter's; the rows land and the cards turn, but "See who won" works at once and any tap finishes the motion. 11: the winners' band, "You won."/"You lost.", every player's card (dealt, arrow, final) with "Won"/"Died", one compact line each, the center, then the night open (the red line: where each Werewolf card went; then a line per step on a spine red where a werewolf card was touched; a tap on the red line folds it; a big table's continues under the fade); host "Play again" and "Edit deck" (play again, then 03), stacked when a label would not fit on one line; guest "{host} deals next." (when the host is at the table) as the message, with "Leave" a quiet word beside it (`TextAction`), never a button; the viewer's own row says "You" (`YouTag`); the night scrolls fully clear of the pinned line, under a tall fade; a failed request shows "Not sent." with "Try again". Both wait calmly (`EndWaiting`) until the results load. `CountView` and `ResultsView` take plain data, so the component sheet draws them too.
