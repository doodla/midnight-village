# Frontend tests

`just test` runs `flutter test --exclude-tags e2e` (everything under `test/`). Two suites live
outside `test/` on purpose and are not part of `just test`: `test_e2e/` (the live-backend
harness, `just test-harness`) and `test_screens/` (the screen gallery and the cold-read pack,
`just screens`, `just playtest-pack`). Server payloads in tests: `helpers/contract/README.md`.

## The e2e harness (`test_e2e/`)

- `just test-harness` runs the deterministic e2e harness in `test_e2e/` and boots (and stops) the harness backend on `:8001` itself. The API runner replays all 29 reference games from `backend/tests/onuw/data/reference_games.json` through the two-window night and asserts the debug-state oracle; the UI runner plays a subset through the real widget tree with a live WebSocket and writes per-player PNG filmstrips. `api_lifecycle_test.dart` (helpers `harness/live_table.dart`, `PlayerWire.connectLobby`) runs the table lifecycle through real sockets: play again, dropped phones (night and vote grace, `night_results`) and host handover; the oracle's `pending_timers` shows which timers are armed. Rules for harness tests: `DebugTableController.driveAs` is strict (a refusal throws; `lenient: true` is for the toolbar), wall-clock checks use `TimeBox`, waits take explicit timeouts, and nothing is slept on. It is intentionally NOT under `test/` and NOT part of `just test`. Full docs: `docs/dev/debug-harness/`.
- The night is two windows: a player may get a prompt, submit, then wait with no result until the window closes (`submitAction` shows waiting); results arrive together and are held until dismissed; the Doppelganger's look result arrives with her follow-up prompt in the same window. Under night protocol 1 (the old screens) `skip` is the "do nothing" control wherever the server's prompt lists it; under protocol 2 (the rebuilt night) every phone sends one `choose` per window with the stage, and doing nothing is picking "You" alone, on every prompt.
- Shared test helper `MockHttpClient` lives in `integration_test/helpers/` (the web integration build cannot import out of its root); `test/helpers/mock_http_client.dart` re-exports it.
- Protocol 2 through the real widget tree against the harness backend: test_e2e/ui_protocol2_test.dart (from the screens branch): two games from the look stage through the night, the talk (My night held, ready on, off and on) and the vote (pick, lock), then 10 and 11 as the oracle says the game went (`test_e2e/harness/end_of_game.dart`, shared with the end-of-game runner), each tap checked against the oracle (`ready`, `votes`, `vote_locked`). The end of the game, apart: test_e2e/ui_protocol2_end_test.dart plays whole games through the API (the Hunter's chain, a tie, nobody dies with a card moved, the Tanner) and opens one phone at the results, checking what 10 and 11 say against the oracle (who was eliminated and why, the winning teams, that phone's outcome); after the host's Play again every phone opens at 02.
- The whole way on one phone: test_e2e/ui_protocol2_journey_test.dart starts at 01 signed out ("Start a table"), follows 02 as three guests join through the API (the lobby's socket is live, `UiHarness.readLobby`), picks Classic on 03 and checks its cards against the server's `deck_preview`, deals from 02, lets every player play the game through the API (nothing forced) while the phone follows, checks 10 and 11 against the oracle and plays again back to 02. `UiHarness.followTable` and `readLobby` read `/me/table/` and the lobby again in the real zone (a read a pump started runs in the fake zone and never completes); `openShell` drops a game read a pump started.
- `/debug` route (dev-only, gated by const `Env.isDev` so it tree-shakes out of prod; its "Rebuilt screens" switch, on by default, makes a protocol-2 table, off a protocol-1 one for the old game screen) — the debug toolbar: spin up a table, switch player perspectives, god panel, auto-drive. Core lives in `lib/core/debug/`.

## Fonts in tests

`test/flutter_test_config.dart` loads every family in the build's FontManifest (Gloock, Figtree and MaterialIcons) via `test/helpers/test_fonts.dart`, so widget tests, goldens and the screen gallery draw real glyphs for those. Families the app names but does not bundle are drawn in Figtree stand-ins: `Roboto` (Material's default, used by the test theme) and the old look's google_fonts families (until the old look is removed). Build old-theme styles in a unit test through `quietRetiredFonts` to keep google_fonts' load errors for those families out of the output. Delete the retired section of `test_fonts.dart` with the old look.

## Goldens

One baseline set, in `test/design_system/goldens/`, made on Linux. Golden-tagged tests are skipped on other OSes (`dart_test.yaml`): font rasterisation differs per OS, and CI (Linux) is the source of truth. Regenerate with `just flutter-golden-update` on Linux, or from the CI workflow (see `.github/workflows/flutter-tests.yml`). Failure diffs go to `test/design_system/failures/` (gitignored).

## Screen gallery (`test_screens/`)

`just screens [width] [report|strict]` (also from the root) renders every screen in the catalogue from the real widgets, in the app's theme, with images precached, to `build/screens/<id>.png` at 390x844 (2x), puts each next to its mock in `build/screens/compare/`, and prints a summary (also in `build/screens/summary.txt`) of the layout overflows and exceptions caught per screen, and on a player screen (`ScreenEntry.playerScreen`) every run of text drawn below the reading floor (16 px to act, 14 px captions, as drawn after any shrinking to fit) or set as an uppercase tracked micro-label (`test_screens/reading_floor.dart`), and on every screen any text drawn cut short (`test/helpers/truncated_text.dart`, the fit sweep's check: a name or label is set smaller or wraps, never ends in "..."). Text in the old theme's fonts is drawn in Figtree stand-ins (see "Fonts in tests"). `just screens 360` renders 360 wide into `build/screens/w360/`; `just screens 390 strict` exits non-zero on findings. A screen whose builder throws is reported as BUILD FAILED and always fails the run, with no PNG. It needs no backend and lives in `test_screens/`, outside `test/`, so `just test` never runs it (the same structural exclusion as `test_e2e/`); `test_screens/flutter_test_config.dart` reuses the test config, and `screen_gallery_test.dart` there proves the detector on every run. Every screen and state is one entry in `test_screens/screen_catalogue.dart`; `screen_fixtures.dart` (and `shell_fixtures.dart`, `table_fixtures.dart` for 01 to 03b through the app's routes) reaches each state through the server's own payloads and events (no state the server cannot produce). A mock screen the app lacks, or cannot reach, is a `ScreenEntry.notBuilt` with the reason. The catalogue ends with the component sheet (`c-<page>-<ground>`, from `test/helpers/component_sheet.dart`): each mock rebuilt from the primitives next to its mock (08 and 09 are the app's own `TalkView` and `VoteView` on recorded `/me/` bodies), and the state pages (buttons, players, gates, faces) on night, day and paper.
