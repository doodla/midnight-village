# Frontend tests

`just test` runs `flutter test --dart-define=ENV=dev --exclude-tags e2e` (everything under `test/`). Two suites live
outside `test/` on purpose and are not part of `just test`: `test_e2e/` (the live-backend
harness, `just test-harness`) and `test_screens/` (the screen gallery and the cold-read pack,
`just screens`, `just playtest-pack`). Server payloads in tests: `helpers/contract/README.md`.

## The e2e harness (`test_e2e/`)

- `just test-harness` runs the deterministic e2e harness in `test_e2e/` and boots (and stops) its own harness backend: on `HARNESS_PORT`, or a free port it picks, passed to the tests as `--dart-define=HARNESS_PORT` (`HarnessConfig.port`); it stops only the processes it started (their process group, a hard kill after 5 s), so runs in other worktrees go side by side. It clears `integration_test/screenshots/` first. The API runner replays all 29 reference games from `backend/tests/onuw/data/reference_games.json` through the choose API (every phone looks, then each phone with a choose left answers its prompt by `kind`, the day through `stagedAs` with locked votes) and asserts the debug-state oracle, including that no phone with nothing to choose sent a choose (the oracle's per-window `chosen`) and that every window closed by itself once the phones with a choice had chosen (no timer fired). `ui_roles_test.dart` deals reference games 1, 4, 5, 8 and 21 again and plays one phone (the Seer looking at a player, the Doppelganger copying the Robber and robbing on her follow-up, the Hunter keeping his card, the Insomniac, the Seer doing nothing) through the table's shell while the others make the game's choices through the API; it checks the cards at dawn, My night (page by page) and the outcome against the game. Per-phone PNG filmstrips go to `integration_test/screenshots/<scenario>/`; the phone helper is `harness/phone.dart`. `api_lifecycle_test.dart` (helpers `harness/live_table.dart`, `PlayerWire.connectLobby`) runs the table lifecycle through real sockets: play again, dropped phones (night and vote grace, `night_results`) and host handover; the oracle's `pending_timers` shows which timers are armed. Rules for harness tests: `DebugTableController.stagedAs` is strict (a refusal throws; `lenient: true` is for the toolbar), wall-clock checks use `TimeBox`, waits take explicit timeouts, and nothing is slept on. It is intentionally NOT under `test/` and NOT part of `just test`. Where a harness test asserts words, it reads them from the app's own copy (`NightCopy` templates, `board.prompt.kind.go`/`.pass`), never a typed copy of them; a literal stays only where the test means to pin that wording. GitHub runs the harness (the "End-to-end Harness" job in `.github/workflows/flutter-tests.yml`, the same `just test-harness`); the commit gate does not. Full docs: `docs/dev/debug-harness/`.
- The night is two windows (window 1 only with a Doppelganger in the deck): every phone with a choice sends one `choose` per window with the stage (doing nothing is picking "You" alone); a phone whose prompt is `none` sends nothing (`Phone.choose` sends only while `chooses_left` is above 0) and its board sleeps; the Doppelganger's look is read at once and her follow-up turn arms the same board.
- Shared test helper `MockHttpClient` lives in `integration_test/helpers/` (the web integration build cannot import out of its root); `test/helpers/mock_http_client.dart` re-exports it.
- Through the real widget tree against the harness backend: test_e2e/ui_game_test.dart: two games from the look stage through the night, the talk (My night held, ready on, off and on) and the vote (pick, lock), then 10 and 11 as the oracle says the game went (`test_e2e/harness/end_of_game.dart`, shared with the end-of-game runner), each tap checked against the oracle (`ready`, `votes`, `vote_locked`). The end of the game, apart: test_e2e/ui_end_test.dart plays whole games through the API (the Hunter's chain, a tie, nobody dies with a card moved, the Tanner) and opens one phone at the results, checking what 10 and 11 say against the oracle (who was eliminated and why, the winning teams, that phone's outcome); after the host's Play again every phone opens at 02.
- The whole way on one phone: test_e2e/ui_journey_test.dart starts at 01 signed out ("Start a table"), follows 02 as three guests join through the API (the lobby's socket is live, `UiHarness.readLobby`), picks Classic on 03 and checks its cards against the server's `deck_preview`, deals from 02, lets every player play the game through the API (nothing forced) while the phone follows, checks 10 and 11 against the oracle and plays again back to 02. `UiHarness.followTable` and `readLobby` read `/me/table/` and the lobby again in the real zone (a read a pump started runs in the fake zone and never completes); `openShell` drops a game read a pump started.
- `/debug` route (dev-only, gated by const `Env.isDev` so it tree-shakes out of prod) — the debug toolbar: spin up a table, switch player perspectives, god panel, auto-drive everyone but the phone shown (ready, keep their card, vote someone, through `DebugTableController.stagedAs`; fire the pending timer), reset learned cues. Core lives in `lib/core/debug/`.

## The web integration test (`integration_test/auth_flow_test.dart`)

`just test-integration-ci` drives it in headless Chrome through `flutter drive`, with a
chromedriver of Chrome's own version on port 4444; CI installs both. Running it in the cloud
container: `docs/dev/environment.md`.

In a real browser some of the app's work answers in later tasks with no frame scheduled
(this device's token store is WebCrypto and browser storage), so `pumpAndSettle` can return
halfway through a tap's requests: after a tap, wait for what the step ends on with
`tester.pumpUntilFound(finder)` (`integration_test/helpers/navigation_helpers.dart`).

## Fonts in tests

`test/flutter_test_config.dart` loads every family in the build's FontManifest (Gloock, Figtree and MaterialIcons) via `test/helpers/test_fonts.dart`, so widget tests, goldens and the screen gallery draw real glyphs for those. `Roboto` (Material's default, the debug toolbar's) is drawn in a Figtree stand-in. The e2e UI runners load the same fonts (`HarnessConfig.loadFonts`); nothing is fetched.

## Goldens

One baseline set, in `test/design_system/goldens/`, made on Linux. Golden-tagged tests are skipped on other OSes (`dart_test.yaml`): font rasterisation differs per OS, and CI (Linux) is the source of truth. Regenerate with `just flutter-golden-update` on Linux, or from the CI workflow (see `.github/workflows/flutter-tests.yml`). Failure diffs go to `test/design_system/failures/` (gitignored).

## Screen gallery (`test_screens/`)

`just screens [width] [report|strict]` (also from the root) renders every screen in the catalogue from the real widgets, in the app's theme, with images precached, to `build/screens/<id>.png` at 390x844 (2x), puts each next to its mock in `build/screens/compare/`, and prints a summary (also in `build/screens/summary.txt`) of the layout overflows and exceptions caught per screen, and on a player screen (`ScreenEntry.playerScreen`) every run of text drawn below the reading floor (16 px to act, 14 px captions, as drawn after any shrinking to fit) or set as an uppercase tracked micro-label (`test_screens/reading_floor.dart`), and on every screen any text drawn cut short (`test/helpers/truncated_text.dart`, the fit sweep's check: a name or label is set smaller or wraps, never ends in "..."). `just screens 360` renders 360 wide into `build/screens/w360/`; `just screens 390 strict` exits non-zero on findings. A screen whose builder throws is reported as BUILD FAILED and always fails the run, with no PNG. It needs no backend and lives in `test_screens/`, outside `test/`, so `just test` never runs it (the same structural exclusion as `test_e2e/`); `test_screens/flutter_test_config.dart` reuses the test config, and `screen_gallery_test.dart` there proves the detector on every run. Every screen and state is one entry in `test_screens/screen_catalogue.dart`; `table_fixtures.dart` (01 to 03b through the app's routes), `shell_fixtures.dart` and `end_fixtures.dart` (04 to 11 through the table's shell) reaches each state through the server's own payloads and events (no state the server cannot produce). A mock screen the app lacks, or cannot reach, is a `ScreenEntry.notBuilt` with the reason. The catalogue ends with the component sheet (`c-<page>-<ground>`, from `test/helpers/component_sheet.dart`): each mock rebuilt from the primitives next to its mock (08 and 09 are the app's own `TalkView` and `VoteView` on recorded `/me/` bodies), and the state pages (buttons, players, gates, faces) on night, day and paper.

### Loading and error states (issue 10)

Every place the app waits and every request that can fail has a picture in the catalogue: the opening (first frame with no sign yet, the lanterns, "Still on its way.", "One moment" with "Try again", the seat not yet known), 01's Start and Join busy and failing (`01-join-starting`, `-joining`, `-join-not-sent`, `-start-not-sent`, `-no-table`, `-full`, `-playing`), 02 waiting, offline, dealing, deal and leave failing, leaving, 04, 08 and 09 waiting (and 04's and 08's wait failing), 10 and 11 loading, the game offline over 06, 08 and 09, "Not sent." on 04 and 09, and 11's Play again busy, Leave busy, failing and refused. An entry's `frames` is how many 100 ms frames are drawn before the picture (1 for a first frame). 02's and 03's waiting pictures show what the held files draw today.

`just loading-clips [folder]` (`test_screens/loading_clips_test.dart`, `tool/loading_clips.py`) draws four scenes of the real app frame by frame on the test's clock and makes a looping WebP of each, 390 wide and under 1.2 MB (it lowers the quality until it is): a tap going busy then done (Start a table), a join that takes seven seconds, a failed send and its retry (Play again), a reconnect in a game.

## Scenes in motion (`test_screens/scene_frames_test.dart`)

`just scene-frames [folder] [label]` (add `--dart-define=SCENE_FRAMES_STEP_MS=17` to the `flutter test` line for one frame every 17 ms, as real speed for an animation; no strip then) pumps the table shell through the dusk (the deal to the night) and the dawn (the night to the talk) at 390x844 (2x), eight frames evenly from the first frame to `Motion.scene`, one PNG each and a strip of them side by side, into `<folder>/dusk` and `<folder>/dawn` (default `build/scene_frames`), named `<label>-NN-<ms>ms.png` (default label `after`). The clock is the test's, so the frames are the same every run; the pictures are loaded for real before the first frame is drawn. Run it on a shell that crossfades (any earlier commit) with another label for the "before". Unit tests: `test/design_system/primitives/scene_switcher_test.dart` (the switcher: runs and finishes, no fade over a screen, no frame a blend of the two screens or with a pixel no widget painted (read from the drawn frame, `test/helpers/frame_pixels.dart`), a touch mid-scene lands on the new screen, a scene cutting a crossfade short pops nothing, reduce motion cuts, every other change crossfades) and `test/features/game/presentation/scene_shell_test.dart` (the same through the real shell: no gap in any frame, the primary button on show by 60% of the scene, and the table answering before `/me/` for both scenes). The layered headers (04's village, 08's dawn; `docs/design/art/layers/`) are held by `test/design_system/skin/layered_scene_test.dart` (the description is consistent, no layer shows its own edge where a scene starts it, 04 travels no further than its art was cut for, and drawn at rest the layers are the flat picture they were cut from, `flat.jpg`, compared in blocks of 4 x 4 pixels, 08's baked sun left out; a layer comes in from its `from` and rests where it belongs), `test/design_system/primitives/sun_band_test.dart` (08's one sun: on its path where the clock is, stepped with no glide, under the hills, up from behind them when the dawn brings it in) the dusk's moon in `scene_shell_test.dart` (lowered after the sheet is down: still moving against the plate when the sheet is within 2 px of rest) and the talk's own frame having no sun, then the one sun (same file), and the picture-size check in `skin_contract_test.dart` (every layer in the bundle at its declared size, none undeclared). Mid-scene goldens are not kept: a golden needs the art loaded, and the test clock never waits for image I/O; the frames above are the record.

`tool/scene_web_check.sh [outdir]` is the browser check: it builds `test_screens/scene_demo_main.dart` (a standalone web entry hosting the two scenes on the real primitives and art; keys d, n, w, r), serves it twice (the card's hold ring still, and breathing as in a first game, `--dart-define=RING=true`; both scenes are the layered headers, since the demo is built from the real primitives), and drives it in headless Chrome with Playwright at 1x and 4x CPU throttle, printing the `requestAnimationFrame` deltas of each scene beside the crossfade, the slow frames of each of the six runs (the browser's run-to-run spread is about 1.5 slow frames, so compare with a run of an earlier commit made the same day), and the card at rest with the hold ring breathing over its layered village (`tool/scene_web_measure.js`). The demo warms the layers up as the shell does (`SceneSwitcher.precacheHeader`). Headless Chrome in the cloud container has no GPU, so the numbers compare the scenes with the crossfade and are not a phone's frame rate.
